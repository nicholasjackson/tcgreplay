TCG Replay 1.0.0 is the first version in ChatGPT.

TCG Replay rebuilds a game you played in Pokémon TCG Live, from its battle log and your decklist, on a live board you open in your browser. Review it turn by turn with coaching notes on the board, rewind and replay any turn, and practise with drills or a game against the assistant. Sign-in is with your Google account.

In this version:
- Every tool has a title, a description that says when to use it and when not to, and explicit labels for whether it only reads, whether it can replace your game, and that it reaches nothing outside the playmat.
- The assistant can tell which TCG Replay account is signed in.
- A bundled skill tells the assistant how to coach on the playmat: read the workflow's guide first, police the rules from the cards' text, keep known and assumed cards apart, and never claim a move it did not make.
- The full tool list is 37,959 bytes across 46 tools (measured 19 November 2026).

Already connected TCG Replay as a custom connection? Open Plugins → TCG Replay → Refresh, then start a new chat, to see the new tool descriptions.
