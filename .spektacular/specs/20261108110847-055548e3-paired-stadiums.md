## Overview

Delta Reign introduced Legendary Stadiums: two different cards of the same name that must be played together as one Stadium. Players and the assistant can play such a pair on the playmat, see it on the board as one Stadium, and have it discarded, replaced and undone as one. Battle logs in which a pair is played import correctly. Which cards play as a pair comes from the card data, so future pair-played Stadiums work without a change to the playmat.

## Requirements

- [ ] **Pair-played Stadiums**
  A Stadium can be marked in its data as played as a pair: two different prints of the same name played together as one Stadium. Any Stadium so marked plays as a pair.
- [ ] **Play a Stadium pair**
  Players and the assistant can play two different prints of a pair-played Stadium together. They occupy the Stadium spot as one Stadium.
- [ ] **A pair moves as one**
  Discarding or replacing a Stadium pair moves both halves together. Undoing it, and the game log, treat the pair as one action.
- [ ] **Pair warnings**
  The playmat warns, without blocking, when:
  - one half of a pair is played alone;
  - two identical prints are played as a pair;
  - a Stadium with the same name is already in play.
- [ ] **Assistant knows the pair rule**
  When the assistant reads a pair-played card's text, it includes the pair rule, and the assistant is told how to play a pair.
- [ ] **Board shows a pair**
  The board shows a Stadium pair as one Stadium with both halves visible. A player can play a pair by dragging.
- [ ] **Import logs with a pair**
  A TCG Live battle log in which a player plays a Stadium pair imports, and the pair ends up in the Stadium spot. This needs a real TCG Live log that plays a pair; if none is available when the work is built, this requirement is deferred to a follow-up and recorded as deferred.
- [ ] **Delta Reign's Legendary Stadiums marked**
  Delta Reign's three Legendary Stadiums carry the pair mark and the pair rule text in their published data.
- [ ] **Documentation for pair play**
  The project documentation describes how pair-played cards are played on the board and by the assistant.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **Pair rule comes from data**
  Marking a test Stadium as pair-played in its data makes the playmat treat it as a pair, and removing the mark makes it a normal card again. Neither change involves a program change.
- [ ] **Stadium pair played**
  Playing two different Legendary Summit prints as a pair puts both in the Stadium spot. The board and the game state show one Stadium made of two cards.
- [ ] **Pair discarded and replaced as one**
  Playing a different Stadium over a Legendary Summit pair puts both halves in their owner's discard pile. Undoing that action returns both halves to the Stadium spot, and the game log shows one Stadium entry for the pair.
- [ ] **Pair discarded directly as one**
  Discarding a Stadium pair from the Stadium spot moves both halves to their owner's discard pile in one logged action, and undoing it returns both.
- [ ] **Pair warnings shown**
  The playmat shows a warning, and still carries out the action, in each of these cases:
  - playing one Legendary Summit alone;
  - playing two copies of the same Legendary Summit print (Delta Reign 096) as a pair;
  - playing a Legendary Summit pair while a Legendary Summit pair is in play.
- [ ] **Assistant sees pair rule and can play a pair**
  The card text the assistant reads for Legendary Summit includes the pair rule. The assistant can play both halves in one play action. The assistant's playing instructions explain pair play.
- [ ] **Board pair display and drag**
  On desktop and phone widths, the board shows both halves of a Stadium pair in the Stadium spot. A player can complete a pair by dragging the second half onto a lone first half. The single-half warning appears while the first half is alone and not once the pair is complete.
- [ ] **Battle log with a pair imports**
  Importing a real TCG Live battle log in which a player plays a Legendary Stadium pair succeeds. The board then shows that pair in the Stadium spot. If no such log was available when the work was built, the deferral is recorded instead.
- [ ] **Delta Reign's Legendary Stadiums play as pairs**
  On the hosted playmat, Legendary Pyroduct, Summit and Trench read with the pair rule and play as pairs.
- [ ] **Docs describe pair play**
  The documentation describes playing a pair-played Stadium.

## Technical Approach

- **Pair play is general.** A data flag marks a card as played as a pair. The game state lets the Stadium spot hold a pair as one Stadium, and verbs, undo, the log, the board, MCP `play_trainer`, `card_text` and the importer handle the flag generally.
- The pair flag and the pair rule text for Delta Reign's Legendary Stadiums are recorded through the corrections of the card-set-import spec.
- **Known risk:** the TCG Live battle-log format for a Legendary Stadium pair is unknown, and the user has been asked for a sample log.

## Non-Goals

- **Enforcing Stadium effects.** The playmat stays a playmat, not a rules engine. It does not apply Legendary Summit's prize reduction, Pyroduct's removal of abilities, or Trench's double healing; the players and the assistant apply them as with any other card.
- **Pair play for card types other than Stadiums.** The data flag is general, but only Stadium pairs are supported on the board, by the assistant's tools and in the importer, because Stadiums are the only pair-played cards that exist today.
