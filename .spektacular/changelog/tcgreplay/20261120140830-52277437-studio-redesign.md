# A new look: Studio for the board and the website

The board and the website now share one design, Studio. The board has a light or dark interface and a slate, felt or navy mat, which you choose from ☰. It shows HP left, damage, Energy and prizes on the mat without opening a card, and its card backs are navy with the new logo. The timeline lets you rewind to any turn or play on from any line, with controls always visible. Menus say when an attack is enough to Knock Out the target and offer a card's text. The website has a new header and footer, guides grouped by what you want to do, and refreshed pages throughout.

## What changed in this repo

### Design system
- **`internal/design`** (new) holds `tokens.json`, the one source of design values, and generates `tokens.css`, which `web/` and `site/` import.
- **Go tests** check that the stylesheet is current, that every declared pair meets its contrast minimum in both themes and on every mat, that `web/src` and `site/src` have no hard-coded colours or px/rem font sizes, that every `var(--x)` resolves, and that no legacy token name returns.
- **The mark.** `internal/design/mark.svg` is the new mark. The logos and icons in `site/public`, `docs/` and `plugin/assets` are rendered from it.
- **Docker.** The `Dockerfile` copies `internal/design` into the web and site stages.

### Board (`web/`)
- **Typeface and themes.** IBM Plex loads from Google Fonts. Interface and mat themes come from `data-theme` and `data-mat`, set before first paint and remembered under the localStorage keys `theme` and `mat`.
- **Restyled to Studio:**
  - the header and layouts;
  - the mat's player blocks, Bench, Active panel, turn strip and hand;
  - the phone counter bars and header;
  - cards and the card back;
  - the timeline and playback controls, including turn and half Rewind/Play, the seat-coloured scrubber, and the docked drag handle and turn list;
  - the menus in all three containers, including the Knock Out hint, the Weakness/Resistance toggles and Card text;
  - the target banner, count panels, pile views and zoom.
- **Tests and baselines.**
  - New end-to-end specs: `themes`, `header`, `mat`, `cards`, `timeline` and `menus`.
  - Screenshot baselines for every layout and menu container.

### Website (`site/`)
- **Frame:**
  - the header, with Get started as a button;
  - the dark footer;
  - the docs breadcrumb and On this page list;
  - the shared prose, steps, callouts, code and copy blocks;
  - Google Fonts, with a test fixture that serves the fonts offline.
- **Pages:** home and its animated scene, the grouped coaching workflows, What's new, Example, Limits, Privacy, Terms and 404.
- **Privacy page** names Google Fonts and the theme settings.
- **What's new** gains "A new look".
- **Screenshots:** baselines for every page at laptop and phone widths.

## Why

The board and the website had two looks and hand-copied design values. Studio gives them one source, so a change to a token restyles both, and the checks keep hard-coded values and unreadable colour pairs out. Along the way, players get clearer readouts, theme choices, faster replay controls and a better organised website.
