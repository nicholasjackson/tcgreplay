# Wait for the player's turn (2026-10-26)

When you play against the assistant on the playmat, you no longer tell it in chat that it is its turn. It waits while you play on the board, takes its turn as soon as you end yours, and answers your cards mid-turn by itself: it promotes after a knockout, and shuffles and draws for Judge or Unfair Stamp. The board shows whether the opponent is waiting, acting or has stopped responding, and locks your game actions while it acts. If you step away for five minutes, the assistant simply asks "Still playing?".

## What changed in this repo

- **New MCP tool `wait_for_turn`** (`internal/mcpserver/wait.go`). It is a long poll (30 s by default) that reports `turn_ended`, `respond` (with each card used and its text), `promote`, `check_in`, `ended` (with the reason) or `still_waiting`. Each result carries the player's moves as log lines and a `next` instruction. The standing instructions gain the play-loop line. The tool finds its game through a new optional `ResolveQuiet` hook, so a hosted wait is not activity.
- **Presence and turn wait** (`internal/game/presence.go`): one wait session per game manager, plus board activity for the check-in, the acting lock with its self-release, and the opponent status. The manager gains:
  - the `Timing` setting;
  - `Wait`, `BoardActivity`, `Locked`, `Opponent`;
  - `AssistantMoved`, `AssistantDone`, `OnPresence`.
- **Game** (`internal/game`):
  - Change notices carry a kind (`mode`, `checkout`, `rewind`, `undo`), and a mode switch now notifies.
  - `HandOff` builds the shared "Your turn in tcgreplay …" text.
  - `OnCurrentBranch` is new.
  - A response window in the turn lock lets the assistant's seat answer the player's card during the player's turn in play mode, only while the wait has reported it. That includes joining an action the player left open.
- **Board server** (`internal/webui`):
  - Every request counts as player activity.
  - Game actions, closing an action, checkout and rewind are refused while the opponent acts.
  - The state message carries `opponent`, and is pushed on every change.
  - `end_turn` reports `waiting: true` when the assistant is waiting.
- **Board app** (`web/`): the opponent status badge, controls disabled while the opponent acts, and no paste-this-prompt copy when the assistant is waiting.
- **Hosted** (`internal/usergames`, `cmd/tcgreplay/hosted.go`): `Registry.ResolveMCPQuiet` finds the caller's game without recording activity or taking a place, so an abandoned game still expires, and the expiry ends the wait.
- **Settings** (`cmd/tcgreplay`): `-wait-cap`/`TCGREPLAY_WAIT_CAP` (30s, at most 50s), `-check-in`/`TCGREPLAY_CHECK_IN` (5m) and `-lock-release`/`TCGREPLAY_LOCK_RELEASE` (2m). They apply to the shim's daemon, the in-process server and every hosted account. Bad values stop start-up with exit status 2, and the start-up log names the values in force.
- **Guides** (`internal/guides`): replay-drill and review-game wait on the player's seat whenever they hand over a turn, and never ask for "done". Tests pin each waiting step.

## Why

The playmat is the only channel between the board and the assistant. The assistant could not tell that the player had finished a turn or played a card that needs its response unless the player said so in chat. This repo now carries that signal through a tool call the assistant makes, with the board showing and enforcing whose move it is.
