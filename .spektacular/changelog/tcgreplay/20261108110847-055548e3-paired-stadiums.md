# Paired Stadiums

Delta Reign's Legendary Stadiums can now be played as the cards say: two different prints of the same name played together as one Stadium. On the board you drag one half onto the Stadium spot and then the other, and both show as one Stadium. A warning shows until the pair is complete, and a Stadium played over the pair sends both halves to the discard, with undo bringing both back. The assistant can play both halves in one go and reads the pair rule in the card's text. Battle logs that play a pair import too, but their format is still a guess until a real log confirms it.

> Derived from project tcgreplay, spec/plan 20261108110847-055548e3-paired-stadiums. See the project-level record for the full feature.

## What changed in this repo

**Card data**
- `cardset.Print` and `cards.Card` gained an optional `playedAsPair` mark, copied by `Print.Card`. `Card.Text()` is unchanged. The test pool gained two made-up pair-played Stadium prints, `tst-10` and `tst-11` "Twin Peak".
- `cardsets/corrections/me06.json` marks me06-094 to me06-099 with `/playedAsPair` corrections. `cardsets/sets/me06.json` was rebuilt, and the only change is the six marks. `TestCommittedDeltaReign` guards them.

**Game state and verbs**
- `state.State.Stadium` is `[]IID` (at most two). `MovePayload` gained `Pair` and the `state.AsPair` role, and the board view's `stadium` nests the second half as `pair`.
- `Game.PlayTrainerWith` plays, completes and replaces pairs. `Game.Move` keeps a pair together. New standing and per-play pair warnings are added, and a completion is not counted as a second Stadium.

**Assistant and board**
- MCP `play_trainer` takes `pair_iid`, and the server instructions and `internal/guides/play-game.md` explain pair play.
- The board's `play_trainer` takes `pair_iid`, and the Stadium spot shows both halves. A mouse click on a Stadium now opens its zoom, and toasts no longer block touches.

**Importer**
- Pairs are recognised from log lines only: two consecutive same-name Stadium plays by one player, or one line naming the Stadium twice. The same print twice is not a pair and gives a warning. A replaced pair is discarded in one move.
- New synthetic fixture: `testdata/logs/synthetic_2026-11-08_ledjo23_legendary-summit-pair.txt`, marked synthetic in `testdata/index.json`. **No real TCG Live log with a pair has been seen.** Verify against the first real one.

**Docs**
- The site's play-game page, the design notes and `cardsets/README.md` cover pair play. The README also explains that playmats built before this release cannot decode the rebuilt `me06`: they keep their catalogue but take no card-set updates until upgraded.

## Why

The Legendary Stadiums could not be held, played, moved or imported as pairs, so players and the assistant had to work around them. The pair rule lives in card data and the code acts on it generally, so the next pair-played card needs only a correction.
