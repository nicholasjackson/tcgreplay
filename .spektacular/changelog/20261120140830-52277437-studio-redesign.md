# Studio redesign: the board and the website in one design system

## What was built

The TCG Replay board (Svelte, `web/`) and website (Astro, `site/`) now share one design system, Studio, and both were restyled to it in a single release.

### Milestone 1: one source of design values, no visible change
- **Baselines first.** Playwright screenshot baselines were recorded for every board layout (phone, tablet, desktop, the wide landscape mat, timeline docked) and every website page at laptop and phone widths, so the next step could be checked against them.
- **One token source.** A new Go package, `internal/design`, holds every colour, typeface, type size, spacing, radius, shadow and size in `tokens.json`. It generates `tokens.css`, which both apps import. The website's hand-copied colour file is gone.
- **Checks.** Go tests check that the generated stylesheet is current and that every declared text/background pair meets its contrast minimum in both interface themes and on every mat. A stray-value scanner fails on any hard-coded colour or px/rem font size in `web/src` or `site/src`.
- **The look was unchanged,** and the screenshots matched.

### Milestone 2: the board in Studio
- **Typeface and themes.**
  - IBM Plex loads from Google Fonts.
  - ☰ offers a light or dark interface and a slate, felt or navy mat. They are remembered in the browser and applied before first paint.
- **The new mark.** A navy card-stack mark with a replay arrow replaces the old logo everywhere: website, favicon, README, and the ChatGPT listing icon.
- **Header and layouts.** A Studio header and framing for every layout.
- **The mat.**
  - Player blocks show prize pips, Deck/Discard/Lost chips and the top of the discard.
  - Bench cards show HP left and Energy.
  - The Active panel shows HP left/max, an HP bar, a damage tag and Energy, outlined in the player's colour.
  - The turn strip has the turn tag and End turn in the accent colour.
  - Hand cards are labelled with their kind.
  - The phone gets tinted counter chips and a header bar with the turn.
  - A Legendary Stadium shows as one card.
- **Cards.** Face-down cards show the navy Studio back with the mark. Text cards and Energy tokens use the Studio card colours.
- **Timeline.**
  - Every line shows Play from here and Rewind to here without hovering, 44px on touch.
  - Turn and half headings have Rewind and Play, both acting on the turn's or half's start.
  - The playback controls head the timeline, with a scrubber coloured by player.
  - Docked below, the timeline has a drag handle (pointer and keys; the board's cards follow the split), one control row, and a turn list with prizes left after each turn.
- **Menus.**
  - The mouse popover, touch panel and phone sheet are restyled.
  - The attack panel gains ×2 Weakness / −30 Resistance toggles and the hint "N is enough to Knock Out X." whenever the damage reaches the target's HP left. The hint never blocks the attack.
  - A card in hand offers Card text.
  - The target banner, count panels, pile and deck views and the card zoom follow suit.
- **Screenshots and legacy names.** The board's Studio screenshots are recorded, including each menu container, and compared at threshold 0. Every legacy token name is retired from the board.

### Milestone 3: the website in Studio
- **Frame.**
  - The header carries the mark and Guides, Example, Limits and What's new, with Get started as a button.
  - The footer is dark, with link columns, the fan notice and the credit.
  - Docs pages gain a breadcrumb and an On this page list.
  - Prose, steps, callouts, code and copy blocks are restyled.
  - The tests allow exactly Google's two font hosts and serve the fonts locally.
- **Pages.**
  - Home has a Studio hero, its animated scene redrawn in Studio colours, How it works, What it does, guide cards and the address block.
  - Coaching workflows are grouped into After a game, Practice and Play.
  - What's new becomes a timeline, Example gets copy panels, Limits gets stat tiles read from the site facts, and Privacy, Terms and the 404 page are restyled.
  - Dropped spaces Astro made on several pages are fixed.
- **Content.** The privacy page names Google Fonts as an outside service and the theme settings among the board's remembered display settings. What's new announces "A new look", which needs no refresh.
- **Screenshots.** The website's Studio screenshots are recorded for every page at both widths. The last legacy tokens are deleted, and a Go test keeps both apps off them.

The full project test run passes: Go vet and tests, the board suite (565 passed) and the website suite (298 passed).

## Why it matters

The board and the website had drifted into two looks, with design values copied by hand between them. Studio gives both one source of truth. Changing a token restyles both apps, and the checks stop hard-coded values and unreadable colour pairs from creeping back. Players also get clearer readouts on the mat, a choice of interface and mat, faster replay controls, a Knock Out hint and card text in the menus, and a clearer, better organised website.

## Deviations from the plan

- **Screenshot baselines** were re-recorded with each board restyle, as the plan's approach section says, as well as in the two final recording tasks. Both apps compare at `threshold: 0` with `maxDiffPixelRatio` 0.002.
- **Criteria narrowed.** Several acceptance criteria were narrowed, with the maintainer's approval, where literal "unchanged" or "as before" wording contradicted a deliberate improvement. The maintainer's standing guidance is to resolve such contradictions in favour of the improvement. The narrowed criteria:
  - the timeline's newest line has no Rewind;
  - legacy token names stayed in the tokens while the website used them;
  - the docs sidebar was restyled, and On this page appears only with more than one section;
  - Terms has no design of its own.
- **Spec amendment.** The spec's contrast rule for borders was amended during implementation, and `felt-muted` became #B4CBBD to pass contrast.
- **The Legendary Stadium** was added to both designs at the maintainer's request before the mat work.
- **Prizes.** On desktop, clicking a prize pip takes that prize. On touch screens, the pip row is one button that opens the prize view, to keep the 44×44 touch-target rule.
- **Kept for existing tests and actions:**
  - the fanned hands;
  - attachment cards over the Pokémon;
  - the exact menu labels;
  - rows as buttons;
  - the line text at 12px;
  - the "Footer" and "Guide" landmark names.
- **The Privacy page** had a Studio design but no task listed it. It was restyled in the pages task, and its content was updated in the following task.
- **Layout compromises:**
  - The docked timeline's range is narrow on a 1440×900 screen, because today's mat needs about 540px.
  - On a tablet, the docked board scrolls.
  - Example's panels stack and the Limits tiles sit 2×2 inside the docs layout.
- **Agents.** Some tasks used one implementer instead of the planned parallel agents where the work shared a file.
