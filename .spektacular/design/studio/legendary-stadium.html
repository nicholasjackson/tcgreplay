Legendary Stadium in Studio

A Stadium played as a pair, such as Delta Reign's Legendary Stadiums, stands in the Stadium spot as one card. The two halves are turned a quarter and joined edge to edge. Cards are drawn as colour blocks, as on the Studio canvas; the board shows the real card images.
