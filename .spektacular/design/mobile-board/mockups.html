Touch-friendly board: mockups

These go with the design document mobile-board/design.md. Frames are drawn at real CSS sizes: iPhone 390×844 and iPad 1024×768 (shown at reduced scale). The cards are stand-ins, and the numbered notes refer to the design's sections.

  1. Counter bars take the place of the piles column and the prize grid. Tapping a chip opens that pile, the deck sheet or the prize view.
  2. The opponent's hand shows as a count (✋ 6).
  3. The bench is one row that scrolls sideways. With Area Zero in play it can hold 8; "+1 ›" says some are off screen.

  1. The header shows the card, a one-line state summary and Card text ›. That toggle swaps the sections for the card's full text, so a separate Zoom item is no longer needed.
  2. › marks an action that continues on the board (target mode, frame C). With one possible target, the button acts straight away and names the target ("Retreat to Riolu").
  3. Damage is a stepper. Tapping the value opens the number pad for a total, which replaces "Set damage…" and its prompt.
  4. Conditions are toggle chips; this row overflows and fades at the edge to show it scrolls.
  5. Knock Out is last and styled as dangerous. Today's list for this card has about 25 items; the sheet has 5 short rows.
  6. An empty section is left out: this card has no Ability, so the row is absent.
  7. To close: ✕, swiping down on the header, or the browser's back.
