## The first sign-in

The first time your assistant uses tcgreplay (or when you connect it), your browser opens **Google's account chooser** straight away; there is no tcgreplay page in between. Pick your Google account. The playmat asks Google only for your email address; it never sees your password, name or photo. Then you are back in your assistant, which carries on.

Each Google account has its own game on the hosted playmat. [The privacy page](/privacy) says what is kept and for how long.

## Opening the board

When your assistant imports a game, starts a new one or sets the opponent's deck, its reply includes a **board link**, an address like `https://tcgreplay.demo.gs/board?board=…`. Open it to watch the game on a live board and make moves yourself.

- **In the same browser you signed in with**, the board opens straight away: you are already signed in.
- **In another browser or device**, the board sends you to Google sign-in first. Sign in with the same Google account and the board opens.

The board's menu shows the account you are signed in as, and **Sign out**.

## If something goes wrong

- **"Not your game"**: you opened a board link while signed in as a different Google account from the one your assistant uses. Sign out from that page and sign in with the right account, or open your own board.
- **"tcgreplay is busy"**: the hosted playmat already has as many players as it serves at once. Try again later, or [run it on your own computer](/docs/desktop).
- **The board shows no game**: your game may have been dropped after a while without activity, or when the playmat was updated. Ask your assistant to import the game again.
