title: Review a game
prompts:
  - "Review this game with me."
  - "Review this game with me. Start with the turn where I lost tempo."
  - "Why do you think that play on my third turn was a mistake? What would you have done instead?"
---

Go through an imported game with your assistant as a coach. It picks the two to four decisions that mattered most and, for each one, puts the board at the start of that turn on its own branch, so you see the position as you did.

**It asks before it judges.** At each decision it sets the scene and asks what you were thinking. It gives its view only after you answer, or when you ask.

**It rates decisions, not outcomes.** A good play that lost to a bad draw is still a good play, and a lucky one is still a mistake. Where a better line exists, it plays it out on the board so you can watch it, and it leaves you with one principle per mistake. It notes what you did well, too.

The mistakes it finds become puzzles for the [replay drill](/docs/workflows/replay-drill).
