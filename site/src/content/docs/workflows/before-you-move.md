Most lost games have a turn where the player acted before asking three questions. This drill practises asking them, in order, on a real position from your game, until it is a habit:

1. **Their turn:** what will the opponent knock out next turn, and for how many prizes?
2. **My knockout:** what can I knock out this turn, and does the damage really reach?
3. **Can I act:** is my attacker Active with its Energy, or is this a setup turn?

Then **spend last**: gusts, boosts and hand-shuffling Supporters wait until all three have answers.

The assistant asks one question at a time and checks each answer against the board and the card text, showing the sums when a number is wrong. A session is three to five positions, and it ends with the question you missed most.
