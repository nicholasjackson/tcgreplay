title: The opponent's likely deck
prompts:
  - "What was my opponent probably playing? Build their likely list."
  - "Show me the list you assumed for my opponent, and which cards the log actually proves."
  - "I think they were playing two Boss's Orders, not one. Update their list."
---

A battle log only shows the cards your opponent played. For rewinding and "what could they have had?" questions to be realistic, the playmat needs a full 60-card list for them. The assistant builds one: it names the opponent's archetype from what they played, starts from a typical list for it, and makes sure every card the log proves is in it.

The import does this for you. Ask to see the list, question it, or correct it if you know better. Cards the log doesn't prove are always "likely", never certain.
