title: Import a game
prompts:
  - "Here's my last game from TCG Live and my decklist. Import it: (paste the battle log, then the decklist)"
  - "Import this game record I saved: (paste the record)"
---

Bring a game you played in Pokémon TCG Live onto the board. Paste the battle log and the decklist you played, and the assistant reads the log, works out the opponent's likely deck, imports the game and gives you the board link. You can then step through it turn by turn.

It also tells you whether the import is complete, and gives a short triage of the game so you know whether it's worth a full review. Then it asks what you'd like to do next: analyse the game turn by turn, practise one critical decision straight away, or stop there.

See [importing a game](/docs/import) for where to copy the log and the decklist in TCG Live, what a complete import looks like and what to do if lines are missing. To try it without a game of your own, use [the example game](/docs/example).
