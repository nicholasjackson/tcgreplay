title: Play a game
prompts:
  - "Let's play a game. Here's my deck: (paste your TCG Live decklist). You play a deck of your choice."
  - "Let's play. Here's my deck: (paste your decklist). You play this one: (paste the opponent's decklist)."
  - "Set up a position where my attacker is Active against your ex with two Energy attached, and let me play it out."
---

Play a full game against your assistant on the board. Give it your decklist (exported from TCG Live) and, if you like, the deck it should play. It sets up both sides, gives you the board link, and you play your turns on the board: drag cards, attack and end your turn there. The assistant plays its turns and tells you what it did.

**Your opponent reacts as a real one would.** When you play a card like Judge or Iono, its hand is shuffled away and it draws by itself, with no need to tell it. It only stops to think when it has a real choice, such as promoting after a Knock Out, and you can keep playing anything that doesn't depend on that choice.

**It doesn't peek.** The assistant plays its own side only on what that side could see: it never uses your hand or anything you showed it as your coach. Where it can, it plays each of its turns in a separate, fresh helper that is given only its own deck and the public board.

When the game ends, it offers to [review it with you](/docs/workflows/review-game).
